Meal Plans

A meal plan for every kind of routine

Whether you're feeding a family, an office, or just yourself between classes, SufraBox has a plan built around fresh, homemade-style meals and honest pricing.

Family Meal Plans

Dinner sorted for the whole house

Family meal plans are sized for sharing — generous portions of home-style lunch and dinner delivered on a schedule that matches how your household actually eats. No more last-minute "what's for dinner" scrambles on a weeknight.

Generous Portions

Family-size boxes built to serve multiple people per meal.

Flexible Frequency

Choose daily dinner, a few days a week, or weekends only.

Kid-Friendly Options

Balanced, familiar flavors that work for both adults and children.

Office Meal Plans

Lunch, handled for the whole team

Office meal plans bring consistent, fresh lunch to your workplace on a set schedule, with bulk pricing for teams. It's a popular perk for companies that want better lunch options without running a cafeteria. See our full office lunch delivery service for corporate details.

Bulk Ordering

Single scheduled delivery for the whole office, invoiced together.

Consistent Timing

Lunch arrives at the same time each day, so teams can plan around it.

Menu Variety

Rotating weekly menu keeps office lunch from feeling repetitive.

Student Meal Plans

Real food between classes and deadlines

Student meal plans are built for tighter budgets and unpredictable schedules — smaller, flexible commitments so you can eat properly without cooking or overspending on takeout.

Lower Commitment

Short-term and weekday-only options available.

Student-Friendly Pricing

Priced to fit a student budget without cutting portion size.

Hostel & Shared Housing Delivery

Delivery coordinated for shared addresses and hostels near served areas.

Healthy Meal Plans

Balanced meals without the extra effort

Healthy meal plans focus on portion control and balanced macros — quality protein, whole ingredients, and vegetables — without sacrificing the comforting taste of home cooking.

Portion Controlled

Sized to support consistent eating habits, not oversized restaurant portions.

Balanced Macros

Protein, carbs, and vegetables planned into every box.

Whole Ingredients

Fresh vegetables and quality protein sources, prepared simply.

Budget Meal Plans

Affordable meals, still made fresh

Budget meal plans prove that affordable doesn't have to mean low-quality. Simpler recipes and larger order volumes keep pricing accessible while ingredients and preparation standards stay the same.

Everyday Pricing

Designed for daily use without straining a monthly food budget.

No Hidden Fees
