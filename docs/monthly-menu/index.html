Monthly Meal Plan

One subscription, a full month of meals

The SufraBox monthly plan takes meal planning off your plate entirely — fresh lunch and dinner delivered every day for a full month, at better value than ordering meal by meal.

How the Monthly Plan Works

Set it up once, eat well all month

  • Pick your meals

    Choose lunch, dinner, or both — daily, weekdays only, or a custom schedule.

    Meals arrive fresh each day at your confirmed delivery window — no reordering needed.
