Cookie Policy

How SufraBox uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

Last updated: July 2026

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function properly and remember basic preferences between visits.

How We Use Cookies

The SufraBox website may use cookies to:

  • Remember your display preferences between visits
  • Understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it
  • Keep our website secure and functioning correctly

We do not use cookies to sell your personal data.

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers let you control or disable cookies through their settings. Disabling cookies may affect how some parts of this website function, but will not prevent you from browsing our menu or contacting us.

Third-Party Cookies

Some fonts and embedded content on this site are served by third-party providers (such as Google Fonts), which may set their own cookies subject to their respective privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Questions

For questions about this Cookie Policy, see our Privacy Policy or contact us at hello@sufrabox.com.
