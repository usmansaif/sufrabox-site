How It Works

From sign-up to your doorstep

Getting fresh milk delivered daily takes four simple steps.

  1. Subscribe to a plan

    Choose morning, evening, twice-daily, or bulk supply, and confirm your delivery address.

  2. We schedule your route

    Your delivery slot is added to our daily delivery schedule, generated automatically each day.

  3. Fresh milk, delivered

    Our staff deliver chilled, hygienically packed milk to your door - morning and/or evening.

  4. One monthly bill

    At month's end, you get one invoice based on your actual deliveries - pay by cash or bank transfer.

Monthly Billing
