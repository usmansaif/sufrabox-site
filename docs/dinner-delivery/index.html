Dinner Delivery Service

Come home to dinner that's already ready

SufraBox's dinner delivery service brings warm, homestyle evening meals to your door across Lahore - comforting flavors, freshly prepared, no cooking required after a long day.

Why choose SufraBox for dinner delivery

Evenings are when home cooking matters most - and when there's the least energy left to do it. SufraBox's dinner delivery service is built around that reality: a proper, warm, homestyle dinner that arrives ready to eat in Gulberg, DHA, Model Town, and beyond, made the way a home-cooked dinner should taste.

Each dinner box is freshly prepared the same day, with recipes rooted in comforting, familiar flavors rather than heavy restaurant-style cooking. Portions are sized generously, and family-style options are available for households that want to share.

Who it's for

  • Working professionals who want a proper dinner without cooking after work.
  • Families looking for consistent, homestyle evening meals - see our family meal plans.
  • Anyone who wants comfort food without the cleanup.

What You Get

Every dinner box includes

Homestyle Recipes

Warm, familiar flavors rooted in home cooking, not restaurant menus.

Freshly Prepared

Cooked same-day, ready for a relaxed end to the day.

Family-Size Options

Generous portions available for sharing across the table.

Check tonight's options on the weekly menu, or lock in evenings with a monthly plan. Family dinner delivery is available across Johar Town, Bahria Town, and Wapda Town too.
