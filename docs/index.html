Our Story

Pure dairy, made the traditional way

SufraBox began with a simple idea: getting genuinely pure dairy shouldn't mean a guessing game about quality. Butter, khoya and desi ghee should taste the way they used to - rich, fresh, and honest.

Every pack is made from fresh dairy sourced from trusted farms, handled hygienically and packed with care. No shortcuts, no adulteration, no compromise on purity.

Today SufraBox offers fresh butter, khoya and desi ghee in clean 1 kg packs - so the only thing you need to do is enjoy them.
