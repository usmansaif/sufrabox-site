Our Story

100% pure milk, brought to your door

SufraBox began with a simple idea: getting genuinely pure milk in Lahore shouldn't mean a trip to the store, a guessing game about quality, or juggling cash with a milkman. It should just arrive - fresh, sealed, and on time.

Every litre we deliver is sourced fresh, chilled quickly, and hygienically packed before it ever leaves for your neighborhood. No shortcuts, no adulteration, no compromise on purity.

Today SufraBox runs a full delivery platform behind the scenes - from route planning to billing - so homes and shops across Lahore's posh areas get milk they can trust, every single morning and evening.
