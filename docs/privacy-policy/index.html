Privacy Policy

How SufraBox collects, uses, and protects your information when you order meals from us.

Last updated: July 2026

Information We Collect

When you place an order, contact us, or sign up for a meal plan, SufraBox collects information such as your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, and payment preference. We only collect what's needed to process and deliver your order.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and deliver your lunch, dinner, or subscription orders
  • Contact you about your order, delivery schedule, or account
  • Improve our menu, service areas, and customer experience
  • Respond to inquiries submitted through our contact form

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Information Sharing

Your delivery details are shared only with the SufraBox team members and delivery staff needed to fulfil your order. We may disclose information if required by law.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve your browsing experience. See our Cookie Policy for details.

Your Rights

You can request to review, update, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us at hello@sufrabox.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, reach out via our contact page or email hello@sufrabox.com.
