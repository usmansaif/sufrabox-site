Privacy Policy

How SufraBox collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website and products.

Last updated: August 2026

Information We Collect

When you contact us or place an order, we collect information such as your name, phone number, email address, and payment preference. We only collect what's needed to respond to you and process your order.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your questions and process your orders
  • Generate accurate monthly invoices and track your payment history
  • Contact you about your order or enquiry
  • Improve our products and service
  • Respond to inquiries submitted through our contact form

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Information Sharing

Your details are shared only with the SufraBox team members needed to fulfil your order. We may disclose information if required by law.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure across our website. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve your browsing experience and remember your preferences.

Your Rights

You can request to review, update, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us at hello@sufrabox.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, reach out via our contact page or email hello@sufrabox.com.
