Refund & Cancellation Policy

How refunds, pauses, and cancellations are handled for SufraBox orders and subscription plans.

Last updated: July 2026

Single Orders

If there is a genuine quality issue with a single meal order — such as an incorrect item or a food safety concern — contact us within 2 hours of delivery. We will review the issue and offer a replacement meal or refund where appropriate.

Weekly & Monthly Plan Cancellations

Weekly and monthly plans can be cancelled with at least 48 hours' notice before your next billing or delivery cycle. Cancellations made after a cycle has started are not eligible for a refund on that cycle, but no further deliveries or charges will occur once cancellation is confirmed.

Pausing a Plan

You can pause a weekly or monthly plan — for travel, illness, or any other reason — by notifying us at least 24 hours in advance. Paused days are added back to your plan and do not count against your remaining deliveries.

Refund Timeline

Approved refunds for bank transfer payments are processed within 5–7 business days. Cash-on-delivery orders eligible for a refund will be credited toward your next order unless a bank transfer refund is specifically requested.

Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds are not issued for:

  • Change of mind after a meal has already been delivered and accepted
  • Missed deliveries due to incorrect address or unavailability at the delivery time, where the customer was notified in advance
  • Dietary reactions to ingredients that were disclosed on the order or menu

How to Request a Cancellation or Refund

Contact us via our contact page, phone, or hello@sufrabox.com with your order or plan details, and we'll confirm next steps within one business day.
