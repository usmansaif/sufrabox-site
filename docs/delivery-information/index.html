Delivery areas, ordering, and payment — explained

Everything you need to know before your first order: where we deliver, how ordering works, how to pay, and how the subscription process runs.

Delivery Areas

How Ordering Works

From first message to your first meal

  • Get in touch

    Call, message, or fill out our contact form with your delivery area and meal preference.

  • Pick a plan

    Choose daily, weekly, or monthly — see our meal plans for family, office, student, and budget options.

  • Confirm your schedule

    We confirm your delivery window, start date, and payment method.

  • Meals start arriving

    Fresh lunch and/or dinner delivered on your agreed schedule, starting from day one.

Payment Methods

Simple, transparent payment

Cash on Delivery

Pay per delivery in cash, ideal for daily or short-term orders.

Bank Transfer

Convenient for weekly and monthly subscription plans, paid upfront.

No Hidden Fees

Pricing is confirmed upfront with every plan — no surprise charges.

Subscription Process

Managing your weekly or monthly plan

Once your weekly or monthly plan is active, meals are delivered automatically on your agreed schedule — no need to reorder each day.

  • ✓ Adjust portion size or delivery frequency any time with notice
  • ✓ Pause your plan for travel or short breaks
  • ✓ Renew automatically at the end of each cycle, or switch plans
  • ✓ Cancel with notice — see our Refund & Cancellation Policy
