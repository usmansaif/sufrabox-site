10 Healthy Lunch Ideas for Busy Workdays

By the SufraBox Team Updated July 2026 6 min read

The 3pm slump is usually a lunch problem, not a willpower problem. A lunch that's too heavy, too light, or too processed will drag your afternoon down no matter how well you slept. Here are ten lunch ideas — home-style and balanced — built to actually keep you going.

1. Grilled chicken with rice and vegetables

A reliable base: lean protein, a modest carb portion, and vegetables for fibre. Simple, filling, and easy to make consistent.

2. Chana masala with roti

A vegetarian option built on chickpeas — high in protein and fibre, paired with a single roti instead of a heavy carb-loaded plate.

3. Vegetable pulao with yogurt

Lighter than a heavy biryani, with yogurt adding protein and helping digestion through the afternoon.

4. Beef qeema with a small portion of rice

Protein-forward and satisfying without being excessive — pair with a side salad to round it out.

5. Daal chawal with seasonal sabzi

A classic, balanced combination — lentils for plant protein, rice for energy, and vegetables for volume and nutrients.

6. Grilled fish with vegetables

A lighter option for days you want something easy to digest, still filling enough to carry you to evening.

7. Chicken karahi with a small roti

A flavorful option that still fits a balanced lunch when portioned sensibly, rather than eaten alongside naan and rice together.

8. Mixed vegetable curry with roti

A fibre-rich, lighter lunch option for days you're eating a heavier dinner later.

9. Chicken biryani with raita (smaller portion)

Biryani doesn't have to be off the table — a controlled portion with cooling raita keeps it from being a heavy, sleep-inducing lunch.

10. Aloo gosht with rice

A comforting, protein-and-carb combination that works well as an occasional heartier lunch.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a lunch "healthy" for a workday?

A healthy workday lunch balances protein, complex carbs, and vegetables in a moderate portion — enough to sustain energy without causing an afternoon slump.

Is it better to cook lunch or order it?

Both work if the meal is balanced and freshly prepared. A reliable lunch delivery service can save time while still delivering the same nutritional benefits as cooking at home.
