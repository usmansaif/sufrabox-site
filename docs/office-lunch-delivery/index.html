Office & Corporate Meal Service

Better lunch for your whole team

SufraBox's office lunch delivery and corporate meal service brings fresh, homemade-style meals to workplaces across Lahore - scheduled, consistent, and priced for teams.

A better lunch perk for your team

Office lunch is usually one of two things: expensive restaurant delivery or a repetitive, low-effort routine. SufraBox's corporate meal service offers a third option - fresh, home-cooked meals delivered on a set schedule, at pricing that makes sense for teams.

We handle recurring orders for offices of any size, with a single delivery window and consolidated invoicing, so your team gets consistent lunch without anyone managing individual orders every day.

How office ordering works

Set your team's headcount, preferred delivery time, and any dietary notes once - SufraBox handles the rest. Orders can run daily, weekdays only, or on a custom schedule that matches your office routine. See our full ordering process for details.
