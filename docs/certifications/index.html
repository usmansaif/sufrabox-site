Licences & Compliance

Regulated, inspected and accountable

Milk is a daily essential, so we work within the food safety, environmental and quality rules that apply to dairy businesses in Punjab, Pakistan.

Food Safety

Punjab Food Authority (PFA)

Food business licence and regular hygiene inspections for milk handling, chilling, packing and transport in Punjab.

Environment

Punjab EPA

Environmental compliance for our operations, covering waste handling and responsible disposal of packaging and wastewater.

Standards

PSQCA

Pakistan Standards and Quality Control Authority standards for the quality, labelling and safety of packaged milk.

Dairy Regulation

Sourcing from registered dairy farms, with animal health and milk collection practices that follow provincial guidelines.
