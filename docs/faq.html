Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SufraBox

Questions about ordering, meal plans, delivery areas, payment, and cancellations — answered in one place.

SufraBox is a homemade-style meal delivery service in Lahore, providing fresh lunch and dinner boxes prepared daily with quality ingredients.

SufraBox currently delivers across Gulberg, DHA Lahore, Johar Town, Model Town, Bahria Town Lahore, and Wapda Town, with more areas added over time. See our delivery information page for details.

You can order by calling, messaging, or emailing SufraBox directly, or by filling out the form on our contact page. We'll confirm your meal plan and delivery schedule.

Yes, SufraBox offers daily, weekly, and monthly meal plans for lunch, dinner, or both, including options for families, offices, and students on our meal plans page.

Yes, SufraBox provides office lunch delivery and corporate meal service with bulk ordering and scheduled delivery for teams.

SufraBox accepts cash on delivery and bank transfer. Payment details are confirmed when you set up your plan — see delivery information for more.

Yes, weekly and monthly plans can be paused or cancelled with advance notice. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy for full details.

Yes, SufraBox offers bulk food delivery and event catering for gatherings, office events, and family occasions.

Yes, every SufraBox meal is prepared fresh the same day using quality ingredients — nothing is cooked days in advance. Learn more on our homemade food delivery page.

Yes, SufraBox offers flexible plans, and we can adjust portion sizes and delivery frequency based on your household or office needs. Contact us to set one up.
