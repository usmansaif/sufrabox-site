Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SufraBox

Questions about delivery timing, plans, service areas, billing, and skipping deliveries - answered in one place.

SufraBox is a daily milk delivery service in Lahore, providing 100% pure, farm-fresh milk to homes and shops with morning and evening delivery.

SufraBox delivers across DHA, Gulberg, Johar Town, Bahria Town, Model Town, Wapda Town, Cantonment, and Ferozepur Road, with more areas added over time. See our services page for details.

You can subscribe by calling, messaging, or emailing SufraBox directly, or by filling out the form on our contact page. We'll confirm your delivery plan and schedule.

Yes, SufraBox offers morning, evening, and twice-daily delivery plans for homes, and bulk supply plans for shops. See our delivery plans on the homepage.

Yes, SufraBox provides wholesale daily milk supply for shops, bakeries, and restaurants, with a dedicated shop dashboard and consolidated invoicing through our shop owner platform.

SufraBox uses simple monthly billing - one invoice covering your daily deliveries, generated automatically by our platform. See delivery services for more.

Yes, customers can skip an upcoming delivery from their account before the daily cutoff time, and your monthly bill adjusts automatically.

Yes, every batch is sourced fresh from trusted dairy farms, chilled quickly, and checked for purity before it's packed for delivery - no water or additives. Learn more on our about page.

SufraBox accepts cash on delivery and bank transfer, tracked against your monthly invoice through our platform.

Yes, every customer gets access to their delivery history, current balance, invoices, and payment history through the SufraBox customer app. Contact us to set one up.
