Event Catering & Bulk Orders

Homestyle catering for your next event

From office gatherings to family occasions, SufraBox provides bulk food delivery and event catering across Lahore - the same fresh, homemade quality, at scale.

Catering that still tastes homemade

Most catering trades taste for scale. SufraBox's event catering service is built to avoid that trade-off - the same fresh ingredients and home-style recipes used in our daily meal service, prepared in bulk for gatherings, office events, and family occasions.

Whether it's a small office celebration or a larger family event in Gulberg, DHA, Johar Town, Model Town, Bahria Town, or Wapda Town, we plan portions, menu variety, and delivery timing around your event so food arrives fresh and ready to serve.

Events we cater

  • Office celebrations, meetings, and team events
  • Family gatherings and small home occasions
  • Recurring bulk orders for institutions and hostels

How Catering Works
