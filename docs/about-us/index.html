Our Story

Why SufraBox exists

SufraBox started with a simple frustration: good food shouldn't mean choosing between "fast" and "actually homemade." Restaurant delivery felt heavy and repetitive, and cooking every single day wasn't realistic for busy professionals, students, and families across Lahore.

So we built a meal service around home-style cooking — fresh lunch and dinner boxes prepared daily, with the kind of balanced, comforting flavor you'd expect from your own kitchen, not a commercial kitchen.

What began as meals for a small group of neighbours has grown into a daily service trusted by hundreds of households and offices across Gulberg, DHA, Johar Town, Model Town, Bahria Town, and Wapda Town — without losing the home-cooked standard we started with.

What Drives Us

Making quality meals accessible while bringing the comfort of homemade food to everyday life.

Our Vision

Our Values
