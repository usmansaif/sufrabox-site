Our Story

Why SufraBox exists

SufraBox started with a simple frustration: finding genuinely pure milk in Lahore meant either a daily trip to the store or trusting a milkman with no real accountability. Neither option was reliable, and neither scaled to shops that needed consistent supply.

So we built a milk delivery service around a proper platform - fresh milk sourced daily, chilled and hygienically packed, delivered on fixed morning and evening schedules, with simple monthly billing instead of daily cash.

What began as milk for a small group of neighbours has grown into a daily service trusted by hundreds of households and shops across DHA, Gulberg, Johar Town, Model Town, Bahria Town, and Wapda Town - without losing the purity standard we started with.
